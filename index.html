I'm Abdullah Iqbal, a video editor and the founder of MUGHEX Corporation, based in Lahore, Pakistan. I specialize in podcast editing and short-form content for platforms like Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts.

I use Adobe Premiere Pro and CapCut to transform raw footage into engaging content through precise cuts, compelling hooks, dynamic captions, well-integrated B-roll, smooth transitions, sound design, and visual storytelling.

My focus is on creating polished videos with strong pacing that capture attention and communicate ideas effectively. I'm continuously improving my editing skills and building my portfolio through independent projects.

I'm open to remote freelance, part-time, and project-based opportunities to help creators and businesses turn their raw footage into engaging video content.
